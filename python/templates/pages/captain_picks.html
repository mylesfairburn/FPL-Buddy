How to use this

The gap matters more than the order. Two players projected within a couple of tenths of each other are, as far as this model is concerned, the same pick. The behind figure is there so that reads as "either of these" rather than as first and second place.

Ownership is a separate question. Captaining the most-owned player is the low-variance choice: when he blanks, so does everyone else. Captaining a differential is how you gain rank and how you lose it. This page ranks on points only, so if rank is what you care about, read the ownership column as the other half of the decision.

The projections are wrong about individuals most weeks. They are useful for comparing players, poor as a forecast of any single score — and how wrong they have actually been is published on the accuracy page rather than described.
