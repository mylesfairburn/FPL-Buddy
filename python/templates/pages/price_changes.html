FPL price changes tonight

Fantasy Premier League moves prices overnight, at about 01:30. These are the players closest to a rise or a fall — worked out from how many managers have transferred them in or out since their last change, measured against a threshold this site has learned by watching real changes happen.

How close each player is

Progress is how far a player has travelled toward the threshold since his last price change, where 100% means he has reached it. It is not a promise: Fantasy Premier League has never published the rule, and everything below is an estimate of a number the game keeps to itself.

The two number columns answer different questions, so they can disagree. Net last night is one day; progress is everything since that player's last price change. A player on the fall list with a small positive figure beside him is one who has been sold steadily for a week and bought slightly today — still heading down, just not last night.

What this page can and cannot tell you

Fantasy Premier League has never published the rule. What is known is that a player rises when enough managers transfer him in and falls when enough transfer him out, and that the number needed scales with how many people own him. Everything more precise than that — including every tool that shows you a countdown — is somebody's reverse-engineering. This one at least shows you its evidence.

A player who has not moved yet may be further along than this says. Fantasy Premier League resets its counter when a price changes, and for anyone whose last change came before these readings began there is buying this page cannot see. His progress is therefore a floor rather than an estimate. It corrects itself the first time he does move.

A reading a night is coarse. Fantasy Premier League updates its transfer counters continuously and this site looks once, at 03:00. A player who crosses the line at lunchtime and is still climbing at midnight appears here as one number for the whole day. That is enough to rank who is close and not enough to tell you the hour.

Do not make a transfer for 0.1m. Selling to beat a fall costs you a transfer, and possibly four points, to protect a tenth of a million — and you only get half of any rise back when you sell. Price is the last thing to think about after fixtures, form and minutes, which is what the rest of this site is for.

Players owned by fewer than 1% of managers are left out entirely. Fantasy Premier League reports ownership to one decimal place, so a player listed at 0.1% is really somewhere between 0.05% and 0.15% — the number this page divides by would be wrong by up to half, and everything built on it with it. At 1% that rounding is worth a twentieth as much. The cost is that a genuine differential being quietly bought will not appear here until he reaches 1%, which is better than a board ranking players it cannot measure above the ones it can.
