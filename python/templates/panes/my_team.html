Paste your FPL ID below and this page loads your real squad from the public Fantasy Premier League API — no password, no account, nothing to install. It never writes anything back: every suggestion here is applied by you, in the official app.

What you get for the gameweek being picked:

  • Predicted points for all fifteen, from the same rated pool the rest of the site uses, with each player's next three fixtures and their difficulty.
  • An optimised starting XI — the legal eleven with the highest projected total, and the bench ordered so auto-subs bring on the strongest replacement first.
  • Transfer suggestions costed against your bank and your estimated free transfers, so a move that would take a hit is marked as one.
  • Chip advice — what your bench is actually projected to score before you play a Bench Boost, and what your captain is worth tripled.
  • Live scoring once the gameweek is underway, plus your league positions and the fitness news on players you own.

Your FPL ID is the number in the address bar when you open the Points tab at fantasy.premierleague.com — /entry/1234567/event/5 makes it 1234567. It's public, it isn't a password, and the FAQ has more on finding it.
